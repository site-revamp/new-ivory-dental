New Ivory Dental and Implant Clinic Homepage
Website Overview
New Ivory Dental and Implant Clinic is a private dental clinic located
in Business Bay, Dubai, offering general, implant, cosmetic, orthodontic
and restorative dentistry alongside advanced diagnostic and treatment
technologies. The homepage redesign is intended to help Dubai residents
and prospective patients understand the clinic's clinical capabilities,
explore relevant treatments and arrange an appointment.

The design direction presents New Ivory as a comprehensive,
technology-supported dental clinic, bringing its implant expertise,
broader treatment offering and patient-focused approach into clearer
view.
Three Main Homepage Improvements
1. Clearer hero positioning
   Replaced the generic "Dental Clinic in Dubai" introduction with a
   more distinctive headline and clinic-specific messaging. Authentic
   clinical imagery and prominent appointment and telephone calls to
   action help visitors understand the clinic's offering and next steps
   more quickly.
2. More organised treatment discovery
   Introduced a dedicated treatment exploration section with six custom
   clinical SVG icons, arranged in two rows of three, and clearer
   treatment descriptions. This gives visitors a more structured way to
   explore the clinic's services and identify relevant care categories.
3. A clearer appointment and contact journey
   Made appointment links and telephone access more prominent, included
   WhatsApp as a contact option, and added a two-button sticky
   conversion bar for mobile visitors. These changes create a more
   consistent route from browsing the homepage to contacting the
   clinic.
Design and User Experience Improvements
- Strengthened the visual hierarchy so the clinic's positioning,
  treatment options and appointment actions are easier to scan.
- Used a more structured homepage layout to separate the hero,
  treatment discovery and conversion content.
- Added six purpose-designed clinical SVG icons to make the treatment
  section easier to recognise and navigate.
- Improved access to contact actions, including persistent mobile
  options.
- Brought the clinic's actual services and technology-led capabilities
  into the homepage narrative without introducing unsupported claims.
Project Notes
The redesign is based on the supplied business research and homepage
audit. The clinic's published contact details should be used
consistently across the website. The research identified differing
opening-hour statements on the existing homepage and contact
information; the correct hours should be confirmed with the clinic
before publication.

The HTML implementation documents the three homepage fixes above. Any
additional claims about clinical outcomes, regulatory status, staffing
or performance should be independently verified before being added to
the website.
