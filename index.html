The New Ivory Philosophy

Dentistry should begin with understanding.

Every smile and every dental concern is different. At New Ivory, treatment begins with understanding your needs, assessing your oral health and discussing appropriate options.

Founded by Dr. Tanveer Ahmed, New Ivory brings together implant dentistry, general dentistry and advanced clinical capabilities in one practice.

Our approach is built around evidence-based treatment, continuing learning and making every patient experience as positive as possible.

Our Clinical Philosophy

Preserve what is natural. Treat what is needed.

New Ivory's stated approach emphasises evidence-based dentistry, thoughtful treatment decisions and preserving natural tooth structure where clinically possible.

01

Individual Assessment

Understand the patient's dental condition before recommending an appropriate treatment.

02

Evidence-Based Treatment

Use established materials and techniques supported by clinical research.

03

Preservation-Focused Care

Consider minimally invasive approaches where they are appropriate for the patient's needs.

04

Continuous Learning

Maintain a commitment to ongoing learning and improvement in clinical practice.
